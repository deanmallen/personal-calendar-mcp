Personal Calendar MCP

This is a personal calendar integration tool used only by its owner (Dean Mallen) to connect an AI assistant to a personal Google Calendar for scheduling and planning purposes.

Privacy Policy

This application accesses only Google Calendar data belonging to its owner, for the owner's own personal use. It does not share, sell, transfer, or process this data for any other party or purpose. No data is used for advertising. Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account permissions.
